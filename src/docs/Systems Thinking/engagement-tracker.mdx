## Expanded: Paid Ads Management &amp; Retargeting (Business Plan Core)

**Hypothesis:** Using **proven top-performing organic content** as ad creative **increases ROAS** and reduces time-to-learn.

**Operating Model**
1. Identify winners (PCS): top 10% posts by CTR/ER/Saves  
2. Build segments: engagers, site visitors, add-to-cart, video % viewers  
3. Launch frameworks: **Magnet** (lead capture) + **Amplify** (reach) + **Retarget** (convert)  
4. A/B test: hook • headline • CTA • first 3 sec • aspect ratio  
5. Budget logic: 70% on proven sets, 30% on tests; reallocate weekly  
6. Reporting: weekly lift notes; monthly ROAS &amp; CAC summary

**ROI Example (given performance):**
- Budget: $2,000 (R38k)  
- Paid CTR (from proven organic): ~8% → 8,000 clicks per 100k reach  
- Site CVR: ~3% → **240 conversions**  
- AOV: $50 (R950) → **$12,000 (R228k) revenue**  
- Net over spend: **$10,000 (R190k)** → **~500% ROI**

## SOPs (Operator-Ready)

### Prospecting SOP
- **Owner:** SDR Lead  
- **Inputs:** ICP list, event calendar, scraping criteria  
- **Process:**  
  1) In-person events (daily log)  
  2) LinkedIn (conn → value nudge)  
  3) Lead scraping (ethics + enrichment)  
- **Acceptance:** 100 net-new targeted leads/week added to CRM, deduped

### Outreach SOP
- **Owner:** SDR / RevOps  
- **Channels:** WhatsApp • LinkedIn • Email  
- **Cadence:** 10-day, 5-touch min, channel-mixed  
- **Acceptance:** &ge; 8% positive reply on targeted cohorts; all replies labeled

### Sales SOP
- **Owner:** AE  
- **Gates:** Demo → Sales → Proposal → Contract → Invoice  
- **Acceptance:** Proposal within 24h; close-won or next step within 7 days

### Onboarding SOP
- **Owner:** CS / Ops  
- **Artifacts:** Access checklist • Strategy deck • KPI sheet  
- **Acceptance:** All accesses verified; kickoff scheduled; PM board live

### Organic Delivery SOP
- **Owner:** Content Lead  
- **Process:** Cohorts → PAC → Scripts → Production → PCS  
- **Acceptance:** 10 videos + 30 iterations shipped with QA per sprint

### Paid Delivery SOP
- **Owner:** Media Buyer  
- **Process:** Select winners → segment → launch → test → iterate  
- **Acceptance:** ROAS &ge; threshold agreed; weekly tests logged; budget reallocated

## FAQs

**Q:** What if a client has no content history?  
**A:** Use the **Foundations Track**: discovery → strategy → 10–15 seed posts → PCS → then amplify proven winners.

**Q:** How do we justify ROI?  
**A:** We only amplify **top-performing organic**. Monthly ROAS/CAC + lift notes tie spend to outcome.

**Q:** Can we do “paid only”?  
**A:** Yes—minimum creative pack and landing diagnostics required; otherwise risk is too high.
