# Posting Calendar (Content Scheduling SOP)

The **Posting Calendar** is a core coordination tool for any content-driven business.  
It helps teams manage publishing schedules, maintain consistency, and optimize content timing across multiple platforms — ensuring every post aligns with the brand’s overall content and campaign strategy.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

To create a **centralized, visual schedule** for all social media publishing activity.  
The Posting Calendar ensures:
- Consistent delivery of content across channels.  
- Team-wide visibility of scheduled and published posts.  
- Optimization of posting times for maximum reach and engagement.  
- Data-driven iteration based on performance analytics.

---

## 🧱 Calendar Setup

Use a **Notion database** or **Google Sheet** to structure your posting calendar.  
Each entry should represent one post, containing all relevant metadata for clarity and tracking.

### Recommended Columns

| Date | Platform | Time | Content Title | Hook | Caption | CTA | Status | Notes |
|------|-----------|------|----------------|-------|----------|------|---------|-------|
| Post date | TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc. | Local time | Filename or video ID | First-line hook | Full caption text | Call to action (Follow, Comment, etc.) | Scheduled / Posted / Revision | Additional context |

### Example Fields Explained

- **Date & Time:** When the post goes live (schedule per platform).  
- **Platform:** TikTok, Instagram (Feed/Reel), YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, etc.  
- **Content Title:** File or database reference (e.g., `Week03_Video12_IGReel`).  
- **Hook:** Preview of the first line — crucial for performance testing.  
- **Caption:** Final copy for the post.  
- **CTA:** Desired viewer action (e.g., “Follow for more,” “Comment below”).  
- **Status:** Track workflow stage — Draft, Scheduled, Posted, Needs Review.  
- **Notes:** Add hashtags, cross-platform differences, or reminders.

---

## 🔁 Weekly Process

**Sunday** – Review finalized content batch and write final captions.  
**Monday** – Upload and schedule all posts for the week across platforms.  
**Daily** – Social Media Manager checks publishing status, performance metrics, and updates records.

### Checklist
- [ ] All files linked to the correct folder or content library.  
- [ ] Captions reviewed for clarity and tone.  
- [ ] Posting times optimized for audience engagement.  
- [ ] “Posted” status updated daily after publication.  

---

## 📅 Monthly Planning Cycle

Each month ends with a **Content Review & Strategy Session**.  
The session should:

1. Review previous month’s performance metrics (reach, engagement, CTR, saves).  
2. Identify top-performing hooks, captions, and post formats.  
3. Plan new monthly themes, campaigns, or seasonal pushes.  
4. Update scheduling slots for optimal engagement times.  
5. Archive or recycle proven posts for repurposing.

---

## ⚙️ Best Practices

- Maintain **3–5 posts/day**, diversified across channels.  
- Repurpose content natively (e.g., captions for LinkedIn ≠ TikTok).  
- Keep posting times consistent (e.g., 9 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM).  
- Color-code or tag by **content pillar** (e.g., Value, Proof, Inspiration).  
- Always link each post to its **script, brief, and video file**.  
- Store final captions and hooks in a centralized **Content Archive**.  

> Consistency beats frequency — predictable publishing builds trust and visibility.

## ✅ Success Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Publishing Consistency** | % of planned posts published on time | ≥ 95% |
| **Engagement Rate (ER)** | Avg. ER per post vs. previous month | +10% MoM |
| **Post-Link Accuracy** | Content correctly linked to source files | 100% |
| **Platform Optimization Score** | Native formatting and copy alignment | ≥ 90% |
