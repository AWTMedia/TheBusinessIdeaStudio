## 🚦 Acceptance Criteria (What “Good” Looks Like)

- Hook lands within first 0–3 seconds and matches on-screen action  
- One idea per beat; no filler transitions  
- Captions: readable, accurate, dynamic emphasis only where meaning shifts  
- Visual proof shown when claimed (screens, data, demo)  
- CTA is explicit and native to platform  
- Audio: voice at −14 LUFS integrated (YT), −16 LUFS (shorts), peaks under −1 dBTP  
- Color: consistent skin tones, no crushed blacks or clipped highlights  
- No copyrighted media without license recorded in credits.txt

Pass-fail checklist for each delivery:
- Hook clarity  
- Narrative flow  
- Proof on claims  
- Caption QC  
- Audio mix  
- Color pass  
- Export specs  
- Filename convention

## ✂️ Editing Principles

**Cut for conviction**  
- Prioritize message clarity. If a cut looks cool but dilutes meaning, delete it.

**Pacing**  
- Short-form: change something every 1–2s (angle, crop, caption emphasis, b-roll)  
- Long-form: chapter every 60–120s, micro-pattern of A-Roll, B-Roll, Graphic

**Captions**  
- Word-per-line limit: 3–6  
- Max 2 highlight colors; use emphasis on verbs/numbers only  
- Burn-in for shorts; SRT for YouTube long-form

**B-roll and overlays**  
- Use purposefully: show mechanism, proof, or outcome  
- Avoid generic stock unless it clarifies a concept

**Transitions**  
- Hard cuts as default; use motion only to serve meaning

**Music/SFX**  
- Underscore at −24 to −20 LUFS relative to VO  
- SFX for emphasis sparingly (section buttons, list ticks)

## 🧱 Structure Templates

**Short-Form Template (30–45s)**  
1. Hook: question, claim, contrast, or number  
2. Context: why it matters (one line)  
3. Mechanism: the how (one beat)  
4. Example: quick demo or before/after  
5. CTA: next step (comment keyword, link in bio, save)

**Long-Form Template (8–12 min)**  
- Cold open (5–12s) with payoff  
- Title card (optional)  
- Chapter 1: problem and stakes  
- Chapter 2–3: mechanism and steps (graphics)  
- Chapter 4: case or demo  
- Chapter 5: pitfalls and fixes  
- Wrap: summary and single CTA  
- End screen: 2 recommended videos, subscribe cue

## 🧩 Platform Specs

**TikTok / IG Reels / Shorts**  
- 1080×1920, 24/25/30 fps (match camera), H.264, 8–20 Mbps  
- Safe zones: 250 px top/bottom for UI  
- Length: 15–60s (aim 30–45s)

**YouTube Long**  
- 1920×1080 or 3840×2160, 24/25/30 fps, 16–45 Mbps  
- Chapters in description; SRT subtitles

**YouTube Shorts**  
- Same as Reels/TikTok; keep text away from right edge

## 🔁 Review Workflow

- Editor upload to Frame.io or Drive with review link  
- Turnaround:  
  - Short-form: 48 hours for first cut  
  - Long-form: 4 business days first cut  
- Review rounds: 2 included (minor polish round allowed)  
- Response SLA: Editor replies within 1 business day

**Timecode notes format**  
- 00:12 Add proof overlay

## 🧪 Quality Control Checklist (Pre-Delivery)

- Spelling and caption accuracy  
- Audio pops, breaths, and mouth clicks reduced  
- Color cast removed; consistent across angles  
- No watermark stock; licenses logged in credits.txt  
- CTA readable and native to platform  
- Export meets platform spec and file naming

---

## ♿ Accessibility

- High-contrast captions (WCAG AA)  
- Avoid rapid flashing; no strobe effects  
- Provide SRT and burned captions for shorts where possible  
- Readable font sizes: min 54 px at 1080×1920

---

## ⚖️ Legal and Licensing

- Only licensed or original media  
- Track all third-party assets in credits.txt  
- Music license type and source recorded  
- No claims of results without evidence on screen

## 🧭 Editor Mindset

- If a cut improves comprehension, keep it.  
- If a flourish distracts from the claim, cut it.  
- Every second should either build belief or earn the next second.
