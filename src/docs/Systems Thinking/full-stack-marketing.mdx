# Full-Stack Marketing Process

This process helps deliver engaging, effective content by focusing on **data-driven adjustments** and **continuous improvement**, ensuring each piece performs better than the last.

---

## 1️⃣ Analyse Current and Historical Customer & Social Media Data

Gather and study existing customer behaviours and social media engagement trends.  
This analysis reveals what type of content resonates, identifies user pain points, and highlights behavioural patterns—all of which inform future creative and strategic decisions.

---

## 2️⃣ Develop Cohorts

Segment your audience into distinct **cohorts** based on shared characteristics such as demographics, interests, or behavioural patterns.  
Cohort segmentation allows for **targeted messaging**, increasing relevance and resonance across each audience subset.

---

## 3️⃣ Build Platform & Culture Insights

Develop deep understanding of the platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.) and the **cultural nuances** within each ecosystem.  
Tailor your tone, format, and delivery style to match platform expectations—ensuring content feels native, not intrusive.

---

## 4️⃣ Define Editing Styles

Choose a consistent editing style across visuals, transitions, captions, and tone.  
A recognizable aesthetic strengthens brand recall and creates a cohesive viewing experience.

> Consistency compounds brand equity.

---

## 5️⃣ Select and Secure Characters (Actors) for Shoots

Identify and recruit individuals who represent the **brand persona** or resonate with your target audience.  
Authenticity in delivery builds emotional connection and improves audience trust.

---

## 6️⃣ Write Scripts for 10 Videos with 30 Variations

Craft 10 original short-form video scripts with 3 variations each (hook, CTA, or structure).  
Testing script variations uncovers which storytelling angles or emotional triggers best convert attention into engagement.

---

## 7️⃣ Record Content through External Contractor

Outsource recording to professional content creators or videographers.  
Quality visuals and audio directly influence perceived brand credibility and watch-through rate.

---

## 8️⃣ Edit Content through External Contractor

Use skilled editors to finalize content—applying motion, captions, and pacing optimized for each platform.  
Strong editing enhances **retention curves**, driving higher engagement and shareability.

---

## 9️⃣ Distribute Content Across All Social Channels

Schedule consistent posting across primary platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn).  
Diversify formats for each—e.g., Shorts, Reels, Carousels, Tweets—to maximize exposure.

---

## 🔟 Analyse Post Performance Using Post-Creative Strategy (PCS)

Evaluate content performance with the **Post-Creative Strategy (PCS)** framework.  
Measure KPIs like watch time, engagement rate, CTR, and saves. Identify top-performing content for amplification and future iterations.

---

## 11️⃣ Iterate and Conduct A/B Testing

Run experiments with multiple creative variants (captions, visuals, CTA positioning).  
Continuous iteration and A/B testing allow data to dictate improvement—replacing guesswork with systemized insight.

---

## 12️⃣ Amplify High-Performing Content through Paid Ads

Use paid amplification to extend reach of organic winners.  
This ensures ad spend focuses on **proven content** with existing traction, compounding ROI and reach efficiency.

---

## 13️⃣ Run Magnet Ads to Attract New Audiences

Deploy **Magnet Ads**—content designed to spark curiosity and pull new users into your ecosystem.  
Top-of-funnel magnets broaden awareness and prime audiences for later retargeting.

---

## 14️⃣ Deploy Retargeting Ads with Proven Organic Content

Re-engage previous engagers using **retargeting ads** featuring top-performing organic posts.  
Keeps your brand top-of-mind and nurtures warm leads into conversions.

---

## 15️⃣ Reassess Post-Creative Strategy for Effectiveness

Revisit PCS data monthly to evaluate which hypotheses, hooks, or creative elements delivered consistent results.  
Remove low performers, refine frameworks, and document learning loops for future campaigns.

---

## 16️⃣ Continue Iterating on Content Based on Insights

Apply all performance data and insights to the next content cycle.  
This creates a **feedback-powered content loop**, where every piece builds upon the last—scaling both creative precision and marketing ROI.
