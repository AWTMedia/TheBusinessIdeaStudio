# Content Production System (30×30 Engine)

**Owner:** Alex Anderson  
**Access:** Full Team  
**Platform:** Notion / Google Drive  

This system is the **core operating engine** behind *The Brand Games* content ecosystem—built to ensure output consistency, cross-platform growth, and a tight feedback loop between **creative production** and **data-driven iteration**.

> “Content isn’t king—consistent, data-backed content is.”

---

## 🎯 Objective

Deliver **30 short-form video pieces** and **30 text-based content pieces per week**, all aligned with The Brand Games’ mission to grow Alexander Anderson’s personal brand and convert attention into active community members.

---

## 🧭 Workflow Overview

1. Trend & Niche Research  
2. Script Writing  
3. Filming Raw Content  
4. Editing for Platform Formats  
5. Upload & Storage  
6. Repurposing & Scheduling  
7. Publishing & Engagement  
8. Analytics & Iteration

---

## 1️⃣ Trend & Niche Research (Role: Content Researcher)

**Purpose:** Identify what’s working now—use data to fuel relevance.

- Track daily trends on **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, and **YouTube Shorts**.  
- Build a weekly **Creator & Hook Analysis Sheet** (top-performing angles, keywords, and sound trends).  
- Deliver a **Weekly Research Report** every Sunday night.  
- Upload findings to shared **Research Drive** and **Notion Research Hub**.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- 10+ new trends analyzed weekly  
- 5+ competitor case studies  
- Report uploaded by Sunday 9 PM

---

## 2️⃣ Script Writing (Role: Scriptor)

**Purpose:** Translate insights into replicable creative frameworks.

- Analyze Weekly Research Report  
- Write **30 short-form scripts/week** following content pillars:
  - **Value (2x)** – teach actionable lessons  
  - **Proof (2x)** – show data, results, outcomes  
  - **Inspiration (1x)** – motivational, founder mindset  
  - **Opinion (1x)** – authentic takes on the industry  
  - **Advice (2x)** – tactical guidance  

- Integrate hook formulas (Story, List, Debate, CTA).  
- Upload final scripts by **Monday night** to shared drive.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- 30 scripts labeled by content pillar & hook type  
- 100% uploaded by deadline  
- Tone aligned with A. Anderson’s brand voice

---

## 3️⃣ Filming Raw Content (Role: Mila / Videographers)

**Purpose:** Capture authentic, on-brand video assets.

- Record **minimum 30 short-form videos/week**  
- Ensure:
  - 9:16 aspect ratio, 1080p+  
  - Consistent lighting & sound  
  - Clear framing, dynamic delivery  
- Upload to `/Week_X/Raw` folder by **Tuesday night**

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- 30 videos minimum uploaded  
- No more than 2 technical reshoots required per week

---

## 4️⃣ Editing for Platform Formats (Role: Video Editor)

**Purpose:** Transform raw footage into scroll-stopping content.

- Edit 30 videos/week using **platform-native pacing and effects**
- Add:
  - Motion graphics  
  - Subtitles & captions  
  - On-beat transitions and SFX  
  - Brand-consistent color grade  

**Deadlines:**  
- Upload edited versions by **Thursday night** to `/Week_X/Edited`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- 30 edited videos (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)  
- 95% accuracy in caption/sync  
- Branded color grade maintained
