## How to Use This List

- Pick **3–5 channels** that match your offer and bandwidth.  
- Design **clear CTAs** and **simple next steps** for each channel.  
- Track experiments in a **Notion or Sheets dashboard** with owners, start dates, and KPIs.  
- Keep what works, kill what doesn’t, and **iterate weekly**.
