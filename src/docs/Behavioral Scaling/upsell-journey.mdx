# Master Logical Upsell Journey Map

> **What it is:** Next-best-offer by *outcome*, not SKU.  
> **Why it matters:** Define the few moves that create most of the results.  
> **How to use:** Follow the checklist → ship → review → iterate.  
> **Loop:** input → signal → scale → standardize.

*A cheat sheet for building LTV through “next logical problem” offers.*

---

## 🪜 5 Stages of the Journey

### Stage 1 — Core Problem (Entry Offer)
**What it is:** The initial skill/course/product that solves the *first obvious pain*.  
**Why it matters:** High demand; gets customers into your ecosystem.

**Checklist**
- Clear transformation stated in one sentence
- Timebound promise (e.g., “in 14 days”)
- Minimal prerequisites; fast start
- Proof: 3+ case snippets or demos

**Signals to advance**
- Customer finishes & asks “what now?”
- Repeats content without improved results
- Support tickets reveal the next constraint

---

### Stage 2 — Immediate Bottleneck (Upsell #1)
**What it is:** The *next roadblock* they face once they start.  
**Why it matters:** Without solving this, they cannot fully succeed.

**Common bottlenecks**
- Skills gap (e.g., sales calls, form, technique)
- Tools gap (tracking, templates, integrations)
- Belief/behavior gap (consistency, fear)

**Offer patterns**
- Toolkit pack, templates, scripts
- Implementation workshop or sprint
- 1:1 calibration session

---

### Stage 3 — Optimization (Upsell #2)
**What it is:** Tools, systems, or strategies that *optimize results*.  
**Why it matters:** Move from “getting results” → “maximizing results.”

**Offer patterns**
- Automation bundle / dashboards
- Advanced playbooks & audits
- A/B testing suite with reviews

**Expected lift**
- Higher efficiency, win rate, or margin
- Lower time-to-result

---

### Stage 4 — Continuity & Accountability
**What it is:** Ongoing coaching, community, or subscription.  
**Why it matters:** Keeps them engaged, reduces churn, builds habit.

**Models**
- Community + office hours
- Weekly scorecards & KPIs
- Resource library + monthly updates

**Guardrails**
- Clear success path & milestones
- Churn rescue: quick-win challenges

---

### Stage 5 — Scale & Mastery
**What it is:** High-ticket ascension for those going “all in.”  
**Why it matters:** Captures top LTV customers; creates elite positioning.

**Models**
- Done-with-you / done-for-you
- Private advisory or mastermind
- Licensed IP / partner program

## 🔧 NBO (Next-Best-Offer) Builder

**1) Map the customer journey**
- List the exact steps from “Start” → “Outcome”
- Mark friction points (skills, tools, beliefs)

**2) Attach an offer to each friction**
- *Skill gap* → workshop/sprint
- *Tool gap* → templates/automation
- *Belief gap* → accountability/coaching

**3) Define success metrics**
- Time-to-result, win rate, retention, AOV/LTV

**4) Design the handoff**
- Trigger: completion, milestone, or behavior
- CTA: contextual (“Since you just did X, unlock Y”)
- Risk-reversal: guarantee, trial, credits

**5) Standardize & scale**
- SOPs, checklists, templates
- Quarterly review of attach rates & churn

## 🧪 Offer Audit (quick worksheet)

- **Core transformation:** Is it specific & timebound?  
- **Bottleneck proof:** Do we show why most fail next?  
- **Optimization lift:** Do we quantify expected gains?  
- **Continuity cadence:** Calls, reviews, fresh drops defined?  
- **Mastery gate:** Clear criteria & outcomes?  
- **Risk-reversal:** Credit, milestone guarantee, or trial?  
- **Handoffs:** Automated triggers + segmented messaging?

## 🚀 Key Takeaway

If you only sell the **Core Offer**, you leave **70–80%** of lifetime value on the table.  
Design upsells around the **next logical problem** to extend retention, reduce churn, and scale LTV.
