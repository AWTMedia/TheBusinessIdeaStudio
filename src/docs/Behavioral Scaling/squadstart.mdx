# SquadStart — Team-First Founder Engine

*A US-focused, Gen Z co-founder matching + gamified validation platform*

---

## **0) One-Slide Summary (for quick sharing)**

**Problem:** Solo Gen Z founders try to build alone, burn out, and stall.

**Solution:** Match complementary 3–5 person squads first (Seller + Builder + Product/Maker guaranteed). Run an 8-week, gamified validation→MVP sprint with a milestone-refund **Attraction Offer: “Win Your Money Back.”**

**Why Now:** Gen Z entrepreneurial intent is at all-time highs; existing tools either do bare matchmaking or long, selective studio programs. We combine **high-signal matching** + **structured sprint** + **risk-reversing offer.**

**Business Model:** Cohort fee + refundable deposit (milestone-based refund), “League+” upsell, success fees/placements.

**Outcome:** Teams formed, validated problems, first customers, real traction.

---

## **1) Market Thesis &amp; PMF Signals**

- **Massive intent:** Surveys consistently show ~70–80% of US Gen Z express interest in entrepreneurship; community activity (Reddit, Discord, Product Hunt, X/Twitter, TikTok) is vibrant.
- **Acute pain:** Solo building = overwhelm, no sales counterpart, accountability gaps, decision fatigue. Public chatter is dominated by “where do I find a co-founder?” and “solo founder burnout.”
- **Proven formats:** YC’s cofounder matching (scale), EF/Antler talent-first studios (team before idea), Pioneer’s gamified sprints (engagement) — all validate slices of the model. Few combine **team formation + structured sprint + incentives** in a Gen Z-native way.
- **Willingness to engage:** Gen Z loves cohorts, leaderboards, and outcome-tied guarantees. They’ll invest time/effort when the payoff is tangible (co-founder + validated MVP) and risk is reduced.

## **3) Product (MVP) — Experience Architecture**

### **3.1 Matching (Week 0)**

- **Inputs (12–15 min):** Skills (Sell/Build/Design/Ops/Data/Content), availability &amp; timezone, work-style sliders, risk/goal bands, industry prefs, psychometrics (Big-Five-lite), redlines.
- **Scoring:** Coverage (40), Complementarity (30), Compatibility (20), Conflict Buffer (10).
- **Assembly Rules:** 3–5 members; max two per dominant skill; must have 1 Seller + 1 Builder; timezone within &plusmn;3 hrs.

### **3.2 Gamified Season (8 weeks)**

- **Phase 0: Orientation (Week 0–1)**  
  Co-Creation Charter (decision rules, equity, exit), Idea Draft ceremony (draft a problem space, not a solution).

- **Phase 1: Validation (Weeks 2–3)**  
  Quest-1: 15 interviews; Quest-2: Landing page + 100 visitors; Quest-3: 10 buy/booking signals (LOIs, deposits).

- **Phase 2: MVP (Weeks 4–7)**  
  Quest-4: Smallest money-making unit; Quest-5: 5 paying users/pilots; Quest-6: Retro + keep/kill/pivot.

- **Rituals:** Weekly squad review (30m), League Demo Day, public leaderboard &amp; badges (Market Magnet, Closer, Velocity, Ops Rock).

### **3.3 Risk-Reversal Incentive**

- **Attraction Offer:** Pay cohort fee + small deposit → **complete all milestones on time = 100% refund of the fee** (or convert to credit for League+ / future cohorts). Optional partial refunds for partial completion to avoid drop-offs.

### **3.4 Legal &amp; Equity**

- **Founder Pre-Nup Pack:** Collaboration Agreement, IP assignment to NewCo, Confidentiality, Contribution Logging policy, Exit/under-performance rules (&lt;5h/wk ×2 → advisor vesting).
- **Dynamic Equity:** Quarterly true-ups across time, cash expenses, revenue closed, documented IP.

## **6) Business Model (US pricing)**

- **Cohort Fee:** $199–$399 per participant (test tiers).
- **Refundable Deposit:** $99 (returned upon Phase-1 completion; or include deposit within fee and refund 100% on completion).
- **League+ Add-On ($149):** Mentor clinics, templates pack, outreach credits, extended office hours.
- **Placement/Success Fees:** Optional fee share for accelerator referrals, or revenue share (opt-in) in lieu of cash fee.

**Example Economics (Cohort of 60 participants / 15 squads):**

- Gross fees @ $299 = **$17,940**
- Assume 65% full completion → refunds = **$11,661**
- Net from fees = **$6,279**
- League+ attach 35% × $149 × 60 = **$3,129**
- Sponsorships/perk bounties = **$2,000**
- **Cohort contribution ≈ $11.4k** (before ops/mentors/tools).  
- *Note:* Refunds are a feature, not a bug — high refund rate = successful outcomes &amp; word-of-mouth.

## **10) Risk Register &amp; Mitigations**

- **Flakes/Ghosting:** Deposit + attendance streaks; Week-1 replacement bench; partial refunds to keep stragglers engaged.
- **Toxicity/Mismatch:** Clear Code of Conduct; 3-strike policy; single reshuffle window in Week-1 only.
- **IP/Equity Disputes:** Pre-nup pack; ledgered contributions; neutral arbitration clause.
- **Low Seller Capacity:** Enforce Seller seat; run “Closer Clinics”; provide plug-and-play outreach systems.
- **Refund Liability:** Model cohorts; cap refunds as share of sponsor pool; offer credit instead of cash by default.
