# Instagram Organic Inbound Funnel

A **profile &rarr; content &rarr; DM &rarr; book call** pipeline that transforms organic reach into revenue.  
This funnel is designed to help you build authority, attract inbound leads, and convert engagement into predictable client acquisition.

> “The few right moves, done consistently, create most of the results.”

---

## 🎯 Purpose

To create a **self-sustaining inbound pipeline** on Instagram using organic content and DMs — replacing cold outreach with attraction-driven engagement.

---

## 🔁 Operating Loop: Input &rarr; Signal &rarr; Scale &rarr; Standardize

| Stage | Objective | Example Actions |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Input** | Feed the system with consistent content | Post 1–3 videos/day across key pillars |
| **Signal** | Track which posts generate most profile visits, DMs, and follows | Use Insights and Meta dashboards |
| **Scale** | Double down on winning hooks and post types | Repurpose top 10&percnt; content across platforms |
| **Standardize** | Document SOPs, hashtags, and CTA frameworks | Create templates and weekly dashboards |

---

## ⚙️ Funnel Stages

### 1️⃣ Profile Optimization
Your profile is the **landing page** of your funnel.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Clear offer in bio (problem → solution → outcome)
- [ ] Profile picture aligns with personal or brand identity  
- [ ] Story highlights labeled (Results, Offers, Proof, FAQs)  
- [ ] Link-in-bio → VSL, booking page, or lead magnet  
- [ ] Pinned posts: *Offer*, *Proof*, *Results*

---

### 2️⃣ Content Strategy (Attract & Engage)

**Core Content Types**
- **Value:** Teach a key insight or solve a problem  
- **Proof:** Showcase results, testimonials, or progress  
- **Inspiration:** Build belief and emotional connection  
- **Opinion:** Take a stand; filter your audience  

**CTAs:**  
“DM me ‘START’ for details.” • “Comment READY if this hit.” • “Check the link in bio.”

**Metrics to Track**
- Engagement rate (&ge; 5&percnt;)  
- Saves &amp; shares per post (&ge; 10–20)  
- Profile visits → followers (&ge; 20&percnt; CVR)

---

### 3️⃣ DM Conversion (Engage & Qualify)

**DM Flow**
1. Inbound message (trigger: CTA or comment)  
2. Warm rapport (1–2 exchanges)  
3. Discovery questions: *“What are you working on right now?”*  
4. Value bridge: *“Got it — we help with exactly that.”*  
5. CTA: *“Can I send you a quick link to book a free strategy call?”*

**Tools**
- Saved replies / AI assistants  
- DM keyword auto-replies (ManyChat or IG Automations)  
- DM pipeline tracker (Notion / Sheets)

---

### 4️⃣ Booking & Nurture

**Goal:** Move from DM to **qualified call booking.**

- Booking link in DM  
- Confirmation &plus; reminder automation (24h/4h/1h)  
- Pre-call survey to assess fit  
- Calendar auto-sync (Google or Calendly)  

**Benchmarks**
- DM &rarr; booked call: &ge; 30&percnt;  
- Show-up rate: &ge; 75&percnt;  
- Close rate: &ge; 25&percnt;

## 🧩 SOP Summary

1. **Post high-signal content daily**  
2. **Engage with comments &amp; DMs within 12h**  
3. **DM warm leads → qualify → book call**  
4. **Track weekly metrics and iterate**  
5. **Document all learnings into templates**

---

## 🧠 Review Cadence

- **Daily:** Engage, respond, track messages  
- **Weekly:** Review analytics &amp; adjust content hooks  
- **Monthly:** Identify top 10&percnt; performing posts → repurpose  

---

## ⚡ Outcome

A compounding inbound system that attracts qualified leads, converts DMs into booked calls, and builds audience trust through authentic, repeatable social proof.

> “You don’t need more followers — you need more **signal** in your funnel.”
