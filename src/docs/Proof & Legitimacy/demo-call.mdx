# **Objective**
Run a consistent, high-quality demo call that uncovers goals and pain, demonstrates AWT Media’s capabilities, and secures a clear next step (proposal or onboarding).

# **Scope**
Applies to all first-time demos with qualified prospects for Social Media Management, Paid Ads, and Retargeting services.

# **Roles**
- **Call Lead (AE/Founder):** Runs agenda, discovery, value narrative, pricing, and next steps.
- **Solutions Specialist (optional):** Shows examples, answers technical/process questions.
- **Scribe (optional):** Takes structured notes in CRM and timestamps action items.

# **Success Criteria (per call)**
- ICP fit confirmed (platforms, budget, timeline, decision process).
- Core problems and goals quantified.
- AWT offer mapped to goals; timeline set.
- Single, specific next step agreed and scheduled on-call.
- CRM updated; follow-up artifacts sent within 2 business hours.

---

## **Pre-Call (T-24h to T-1h) — Preparation & Materials**
**Owner:** Call Lead  
**Duration:** 20–30 min

**Checklist**
- Research brand: website, social handles, ad library, recent promos.
- Pull 30-day public metrics signals (post cadence, avg engagement).
- Load **Demo Deck** + **Case Study** slides; prep 2 relevant examples.
- Draft hypothesis of pains (low ROAS, weak signal content, no retargeting).
- Set meeting agenda in calendar invite; include loom link backup.
- Open CRM record with: stakeholders, goals guessed, must-ask questions.
- Technical QA: mic/cam, screen share, sample creative files open.

**Artifacts**
- Demo Deck (latest)
- Case Study one-pager (PDF)
- Discovery Notes template (Notion/CRM)
- Pricing sheet (internal)

---

## **During Call — Run of Show (30–45 min)**

### **1) Opening & Framing (2–3 min)**
**Purpose:** Warm rapport + set expectations and outcome.  
**Script (sample):**  
“Thanks for making the time. Goal today: understand what you’re trying to achieve, share how we drive results with content-led ads and retargeting, and decide together on the best next step. Sound good?”

**Checklist**
- Confirm timebox.
- Confirm decision makers & agenda fit.

---

### **2) Discovery: Goals, Strategy, Gaps (5–10 min)**
**Purpose:** Diagnose pains and quantify targets.

**Core Prompts**
- “What does success look like in the next 90 days?” (metrics & timeline)
- “What’s working now? What’s not?” (organic, ads, retargeting, offers)
- “Where are leads/customers falling off?” (awareness → conversion → LTV)
- “Budget, constraints, decision process?” (who/when/how)

**Capture (verbatim in CRM)**
- Primary KPI (e.g., 2.2×+ ROAS, CAC target, follower growth tied to leadflow)
- Current posting cadence & best/worst content
- Offer(s) & funnel assets available
- Stack (platforms, pixels, events)

---

### **3) Services Overview (10–15 min) — Tie to Their Pains**
**Core Services (message → outcome)**
- **Social Media Management:** Signal content system → consistent demand gen & community.
- **Paid Advertising:** Spin up top organic posts into ads → higher CVR; guarantee **≥2.2× ROAS** minimum target.
- **Retargeting Campaigns:** Warm audiences & recapture → amplify engagement and ROI.

**Supporting Services**
- **Data & Customer Insights:** A/B & cohort analysis to iterate creative and targeting.
- **Cohort Development:** ICP slices to increase message-market match.
- **Content Iteration Engine:** 10 scripts + 30 iterations → rapid testing loop.

**Show-Don’t-Tell**
- Share 1–2 case slides aligned to their niche/pain.
- Briefly screen a creative → metric → outcome story.

---

### **4) Process & Timeline (5–10 min)**
**Onboarding (Week 0)**
- Strategy alignment call; access collection (ads mgr, pixels, brand kit, past data).

**Content Creation (Weeks 1–2)**
- Customer analysis → content plan; scripting, recording, editing.
- Daily posting across chosen platforms; cross-platform optimization.

**Performance & Optimization (Ongoing)**
- Weekly pulse; monthly reports.
- A/B tests on hooks, offers, and retargeting segments.

**CTA:** Confirm the timeline that matches their goals.

---

### **5) Requirements & Collaboration (3–5 min)**
**Minimums**
- Active social accounts; brand assets (logo, guidelines, raw footage if any).
- Commitment to content calendar and feedback cycles.

**Collaboration Rhythm**
- Weekly 20-min stand-up; monthly strategy review.
- 24–48h SLA on feedback & approvals.

---

### **6) Pricing, Terms & Risk Reversal (3–5 min)**
**Pricing Model (example framing)**
- Setup (if any), monthly retainer, and optional performance components.
- Payment terms & methods; cancellation policy and notice period.

**Script (value frame)**
“We tie delivery to measurable outcomes — content that spins into paid, retargeting for efficient conversions, and monthly iteration. Most clients see traction within the first cycle; our floor target is **2.2× ROAS** with iterative improvement.”

---

### **7) Proof & Expectations (3–5 min)**
- Share 1–2 **client success** bullets w/ specific metrics.
- Set **realistic timelines** for lift based on account maturity.

---

### **8) Close & Next Steps (3–5 min)**
**Options**
- A) Proceed to **Onboarding Call** (book now).
- B) Receive **Proposal/SOW** (24h) + schedule review.
- C) Park with agreed revisit date.

**On-Call Actions**
- Calendar-book onboarding or proposal review before hanging up.
- Recap owner, date, and deliverables.

---

## **Post-Call (Within 2 Business Hours) — Follow-Up & CRM**
**Owner:** Call Lead / Scribe

**Email Follow-Up Template**
- Thank you + 3-bullet recap of goals/pains.
- Proposed solution summary (services + timeline).
- Next step calendar link (already scheduled) + asset checklist.
- Attach case study PDF and call recording (if applicable).

**CRM Updates**
- Discovery fields completed; next step & date set.
- Probability/Stage updated; tasks assigned (proposal, access request).

**Artifacts to Generate**
- Proposal & SOW (if requested).
- Access checklist (Ads Manager, pixels, brand assets).
- Draft content calendar (first 2 weeks) if advancing.

---

## **Quality Assurance (Every Call)**
- ✅ Agenda stated; time confirmed.  
- ✅ At least 3 quantified goals captured.  
- ✅ Services mapped to explicit pains.  
- ✅ Timeline & collaboration rhythm agreed.  
- ✅ Concrete next step booked.  
- ✅ CRM + follow-up sent within SLA.

---

## **Objection Handling (Cheat Sheet)**
- **“Price is high.”** → Reframe to ROI: connect to CPA/ROAS targets; show payback math and phased scope.  
- **“Tried agencies before.”** → Contrast with signal-to-paid engine + retargeting, weekly iteration, and KPI governance.  
- **“No time for content.”** → We own scripting, editing, posting; minimal async approvals; can repurpose existing assets.  
- **“Need internal buy-in.”** → Provide 1-pager summary + case PDF; offer 15-min stakeholder call.

---

## **KPIs & Review Cadence**
- **Demo → Proposal Rate:** ≥ 70%  
- **Proposal → Close Rate:** ≥ 30%  
- **Time to Follow-Up:** ≤ 2h  
- **Next-Step Set on-Call:** ≥ 90%

Weekly pipeline review: analyze losses; update talk tracks; refresh case slides quarterly.
