# **Objective**
Conduct structured, client-centered sales calls that move qualified prospects from interest to commitment by demonstrating authority, empathy, and results-backed proof.

# **Scope**
Applies to all sales calls following a completed demo or inbound lead inquiry for AWT Media services.

# **Roles**
- **Sales Lead / Closer:** Owns rapport, discovery, value articulation, and closing.  
- **Assistant / Scribe (optional):** Takes structured notes, tracks objections, and logs CRM updates.  
- **Technical Specialist (optional):** Assists in explaining advanced ad or analytics components when relevant.

# **Success Criteria**
- Prospect’s pain, goals, and decision timeline clearly documented.  
- Alignment between AWT Media offer and client’s revenue or engagement objectives.  
- Clear next step set: proposal, onboarding, or contract signing.  
- CRM updated within 1 hour post-call.  
- Follow-up email sent within 2 hours.

---

## **1. Introduction & Rapport (2–3 min)**
**Purpose:** Build trust and set a collaborative tone.

**Checklist**
- Greet warmly; thank them for their time.  
- Confirm names, roles, and business details.  
- Personal connection prompt: “I saw your latest campaign/product launch — how did it go?”  
- Confirm duration and goal: “We’ll spend 30–40 minutes identifying what’s working, what’s not, and how we can help increase performance.”

---

## **2. Discovery & Needs Assessment (5–10 min)**
**Purpose:** Understand their business, challenges, and targets.

**Core Prompts**
- “What’s your current marketing setup — who manages your content and ads?”  
- “What are your biggest challenges with performance right now?”  
- “How do you measure success — engagement, leads, ROAS, or conversions?”  
- “What’s your ideal scenario over the next 90 days?”  

**Capture**
- Main pain points (e.g., low engagement, inconsistent posting, poor ROAS).  
- Key goals and deadlines (e.g., 3× ROAS, 20% follower growth, better funnel efficiency).  
- Budget range and decision process.

**Deliverable:** A 1–2 sentence summary repeated back to confirm understanding.

---

## **3. Present AWT Media’s Value Proposition (10–15 min)**
**Purpose:** Link pain points to AWT Media’s proven system.

**Core Message**
AWT Media builds performance-driven marketing ecosystems that merge **organic content**, **paid amplification**, and **retargeting**, ensuring data-backed creative testing and consistent ROAS.

**Key Differentiators**
- **Data-Driven Personalization:** Cohort-driven targeting to match content with audience psychology.  
- **High-Performing Content Creation:** 10 tested video scripts → 30 data-optimized iterations.  
- **Guaranteed ROI:** Minimum **2.2× ROAS** on paid performance or optimization cycle restart.  
- **Full-Funnel Strategy:** From creative scripting to ad deployment and retargeting automation.

**Script Example**
“Our approach blends storytelling, psychology, and data. We test what resonates organically, amplify proven content with ads, and retarget warm audiences — guaranteeing consistent ROI growth.”

---

## **4. Walkthrough of Process (5–10 min)**
**Purpose:** Show the system’s structure and transparency.

**Onboarding (Week 0)**
- Strategy alignment and access collection (accounts, brand assets, audience data).  

**Creation & Execution (Weeks 1–2)**
- Audience + competitor analysis.  
- Scriptwriting, filming, editing.  
- Multi-platform posting and ad setup.

**Optimization & Reporting (Ongoing)**
- Weekly A/B testing; monthly performance review.  
- KPI dashboard updates for engagement, conversions, and ROI.

**Outcome:** Client understands what happens when they sign, when they see results, and what’s required from them.

---

## **5. Proof & Case Studies (5 min)**
**Purpose:** Build credibility and demonstrate results.

**Execution**
- Share 1–2 case slides relevant to the prospect’s industry.  
- Highlight key outcomes: ROAS improvements, cost-per-acquisition reductions, audience growth.  
- Quote or testimonial snippet for credibility:  
  > “AWT Media helped us scale our paid ads by 3× in 60 days without increasing spend.”

---

## **6. Objection Handling (5 min)**
**Purpose:** Remove friction, reinforce value, and rebuild momentum.

**Common Objections & Responses**
| Objection | Response Strategy |
|------------|------------------|
| “The price is higher than expected.” | Anchor to ROI: “At 2.2× ROAS, your ad spend covers this retainer easily — and compounds monthly.” |
| “We’ve worked with agencies before.” | Differentiate via iteration system and data-loop testing. |
| “We’re not ready to commit.” | Offer 30-day pilot or phased onboarding. |
| “We handle content in-house.” | Position AWT as system support: editing, analytics, and performance optimization. |

**Reassure:** Emphasize partnership, flexibility, and measurable accountability.

---

## **7. Investment & Payment (3–5 min)**
**Purpose:** Transition from value → terms confidently.

**Checklist**
- Present pricing structure (setup, monthly retainer, or performance-based).  
- Clarify payment schedule (e.g., 50% upfront, balance mid-month).  
- Explain cancellation policy (30-day written notice).  
- Optional incentives: extended contracts, performance-based bonuses, or add-ons.

**Framing Script**
“Our fee reflects the full stack — content creation, ad management, and continuous optimization — designed to yield measurable ROI rather than hourly output.”

---

## **8. Close & Next Steps (5 min)**
**Purpose:** End decisively with clarity and enthusiasm.

**Steps**
1. Recap goals, pains, and how AWT Media solves them.  
2. Ask a closing question: “Would you like to proceed with onboarding or review a proposal first?”  
3. If yes → schedule onboarding call.  
4. If proposal → confirm delivery time (within 24 hours).  
5. Outline next steps: contract → payment → kickoff timeline.  

**Follow-Up Email (Template)**
- Thank them for their time.  
- Recap challenges, proposed approach, and pricing tier.  
- Attach proposal (PDF or Notion link).  
- Include call replay or case study link if applicable.

---

## **Post-Call Requirements**
- CRM notes updated (pain points, decision stage, next step).  
- Proposal link sent within **2 hours**.  
- Follow-up reminder set (T+2 days).  
- Performance dashboard tagged with lead name for tracking.

---

## **KPIs**
- **Discovery-to-Proposal Rate:** ≥ 80%  
- **Proposal-to-Close Rate:** ≥ 35%  
- **Average Follow-Up Time:** ≤ 2 hours  
- **Objection Recovery Success:** ≥ 50% on first attempt  

Weekly review to refine talk tracks, objections log, and conversion data.
