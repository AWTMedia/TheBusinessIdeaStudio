## What is the Value Equation?

Alex Hormozi describes the Value Equation as follows:  

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**  
 [oai_citation:0‡Medium](https://medium.com/%40anferneeck/alex-hormozis-value-equation-the-secret-behind-what-people-actually-pay-for-56f84bd5ce84?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

In simpler terms:  
- **Dream Outcome** – What the customer deeply wants.  [oai_citation:1‡Creator Economy](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/the-value-equation-for-irresistible-products?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement** – How confident they feel they’ll reach that outcome.  [oai_citation:2‡YAP Podcast](https://youngandprofiting.com/alex-hormozi-the-value-equation-how-to-make-offers-so-good-people-feel-stupid-saying-no-e199/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- **Time Delay** – How long it takes to get the outcome. The shorter, the better.  [oai_citation:3‡Creator Economy](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/the-value-equation-for-irresistible-products?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- **Effort & Sacrifice** – How much the customer has to give up (time, money, work) to reach the outcome. The less, the better.  [oai_citation:4‡Fuel Your Digital](https://fuelyourdigital.com/post/how-to-create-a-million-dollar-offer-using-alex-hormozi-value-equation/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Why It Matters

- It explains why two offers that promise the *same outcome* can have *vastly different* perceived value.  [oai_citation:5‡Medium](https://medium.com/illumination/alex-hormozis-value-equation-is-a-true-revelation-b887363cc6b6?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- It gives a clear framework for designing offers: maximize the top side (dream + likelihood) and minimize the bottom side (time + effort).  [oai_citation:6‡Creator Economy](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/the-value-equation-for-irresistible-products?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Example in Practice

Imagine two offers for weight loss:  

- **Offer A:** “Join our 16-week program, lose 15 kg, workout plan + diet plan.”  
- **Offer B:** “Our rapid transformation system helps you lose 15 kg in 4 weeks, minimal effort, proven results.”  

Same dream outcome, but Offer B has a higher perceived value because time delay is less and effort is lower. According to the equation, its value = higher. ## Key Takeaways

- Value isn’t just about features or price—it’s about how the customer *perceives* their outcome, the certainty of getting it, how fast they’ll get it, and how little they’ll need to sacrifice.  
- The bottom half (time + effort) is often where great competitive advantage lies—but many ignore it.  [oai_citation:13‡YAP Podcast](https://youngandprofiting.com/alex-hormozi-the-value-equation-how-to-make-offers-so-good-people-feel-stupid-saying-no-e199/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- Use the equation as a checklist when crafting your offer or sales message:  
  **Does it raise the dream? Does it boost belief? Does it shorten time? Does it reduce effort?**

## Conclusion

The Value Equation gives you a powerful lens: you don’t just sell “what you do” — you sell **why it’s worth it** to the client. Use it consistently and you’ll design offers people feel *stupid saying no to*.
