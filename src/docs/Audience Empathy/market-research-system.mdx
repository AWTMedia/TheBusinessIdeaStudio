# 📊 Social Media Market Research System  
*A Research Framework & Process Blueprint*  

---

## 1) Objective

To create a **systematic process** that transforms high-level business insights into **platform-specific social media data** — providing both **macro-level market direction** and **micro-level content trends**.

> “Research should not just inform decisions — it should shape creative execution.”

---

## 2) Purpose

The **Social Media Market Research System** enables a business or agency to:
- Understand its **market dynamics** (macro trends).  
- Identify **content opportunities** (micro trends).  
- Generate **data-driven creative direction** using automation (via n8n).  
- Document everything in a **Notion-based research library** for traceability and refinement.

---

## 3) System Overview

| Stage | Description | Output |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| **Day 02 — Macro Research** | Collects & processes data from business plan to generate market & trend insights | Macro Trends Report |
| **Day 03 — Micro Research** | Analyzes social platforms for keyword & creator trends | Creator & Content Insights |
| **Day 04+ — Creative Strategy** | Synthesizes findings into frameworks, campaigns, and hooks | Creative Direction Deck |

---

## 4) Transformation Process — Day 02 (Macro Analysis)

### Step-by-Step

1. Retrieve **Market Analysis & Competitive Analysis** from Business Plan (Notion: [Planning](https://www.notion.so/Planning-22d53550947181af925dd8347f9a05cb?pvs=21))  
2. Submit to **n8n Input Form** → *“Market Outline Input Form”*  
3. Wait 30–60 minutes for automated deep research  
4. Receive structured **outputs**:
   - **Macro Trends**
     - SOP (Standard Operating Pattern)
     - TAM (Total Addressable Market)
     - SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
   - **Micro Trends**
     - Platforms
     - Keywords
     - Hashtags  
5. Log process in [Market Research](https://www.notion.so/Market-Research-228535509471807fb693dbaf3aafc420?pvs=21)  

---

### Input: n8n Market Outline Form

| Section | Fields | Description |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Section 1** | Market Analysis, Competitive Analysis | From business plan |
| **Section 2** | Business Details | Location, target market, competitors, growth strategy |

---

### Output 1: Macro Trends (SOP, TAM, SAM)

| Type | Description | Output |
|------|--------------|--------|
| **SOP** | Patterns in market behavior and recurring success formulas | Operational insights |
| **TAM** | Total market potential | Market size report |
| **SAM** | Realistic segment available to target | Sub-market positioning guide |

**Prompt Example:**
> “Analyze the following market and competitive data to determine SOP, TAM, and SAM. Include supporting industry data, forecasts, and verified growth patterns.”

---

### Output 2: Micro Trends (Platforms, Keywords, Hashtags)

| Type | Description | Output |
|------|--------------|--------|
| **Platforms** | Identify most relevant platforms by engagement & growth | Top 3–5 platforms |
| **Keywords** | SEO & content trends within niche | Top 10–15 keywords |
| **Hashtags** | Social visibility and trend acceleration tags | Top 25 hashtags |

**Prompt Example:**
> “Based on market segment and customer behavior, identify trending platforms, relevant keywords, and hashtags with growth velocity metrics.”

---

## 5) Transformation Process — Day 03 (Micro Analysis)

### Step-by-Step

1. Retrieve **platforms, keywords, and hashtags** from [Market Research](https://www.notion.so/Market-Research-228535509471807fb693dbaf3aafc420?pvs=21).  
2. Search on selected **social platforms** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, etc.):  
   - Keywords  
   - Hashtags  
3. Collect and record:  
   - Top 5 Creators (per platform)  
   - Top 5 Videos per Creator  
4. Extract content structure (Hook, Body, CTA)  
5. Document findings back into Notion → *Market Research Database.*

---

|--------|-------------|
| Platforms | e.g., Instagram, TikTok, YouTube |
