# 🛗 Elevator Pitch — Founder Framework
*Clarity that converts in 20 seconds or less.*

## 1) Definition
**Elevator pitch (noun):**  
A concise and compelling statement that explains what you do, who you help, and why it matters — in **20 seconds or less**. It’s not a slogan. It’s a **conversation starter**.

> “This is where clarity becomes currency—where you stop rambling and start resonating.”

---

## 2) Core Template (Fill-In)
**We help [NICHE] achieve [SPECIFIC RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME] using [UNIQUE MECHANISM] — without [COMMON OBJECTION].**

**Rules**
- Be specific (numbers > adjectives)  
- One promise, one mechanism, one objection  
- Speak like a human (no jargon)

---

## 3) Examples (Plug & Play)
- We help **AI freelancers** generate **qualified inbound leads** in **30 days** using **Instagram content systems** — **without** cold outreach or paid ads.  
- We help **fitness coaches** package their **signature offer** in **under 2 weeks** using our **Done-With-You Curriculum Builder** — **without** hiring a designer or developer.  
- We help **podcasters** turn their show into a **$10K/month business** in **90 days** with our **Listener-to-Client System** — **without** sponsors or ad deals.

---

## 4) Builder Prompts (Refine Fast)
- What **urgent result** does your client want in numbers or time?  
- What **roadblock** do they hate (tech, time, risk, cost)?  
- What is your **unique mechanism** (process, system, asset, guarantee)?  
- How quickly do they see **first win** (time-to-value)?

---

## 5) Formats by Time
**20-Second (full pitch)**  
> We help [niche] get [result] in [time] using [mechanism], without [objection]. Example: [proof]. Want me to show you how it works?

**10-Word Test**  
> We help [who] get [result] with [mechanism].

**5-Second Hook**  
> I help [who] get [result] — fast.

---

## 6) Context Variants
**Sales Page H1**  
> Get [result] in [time] — with [mechanism], not [objection].

**Instagram Bio**  
> [Result] for [niche] | [mechanism] | [CTA link]

**Skool Group Description**  
> A community for [niche] to get [result] in [time] using [mechanism]. Weekly [calls/resources]. Join us.

**Discovery Call Intro**  
> In short, we help [niche] get [result] in [time] with [mechanism]. Can I ask 3 quick questions to see if that’s relevant?

**Live Event / Networking**  
> I help [niche] get [result] in [time]. If that’s you, I can show you the process in 2 minutes.

---

## 7) Proof Snap-Ons (Optional but Powerful)
Add **one** of the following after your pitch:
- “Last month we did it for **X brand: +212% in 45 days**.”  
- “Avg time to first win: **7–10 days**.”  
- “Backed by **[tool/process]** and a **[guarantee/risk reversal]**.”

---

## 8) Common Mistakes (Avoid)
- **Too vague:** “We grow businesses.” → *Who? How? By when?*  
- **Too many promises:** One pitch ≠ full services list.  
- **No numbers:** Use timeframes/metrics to anchor reality.  
- **No objection removal:** Name the fear you eliminate.  
- **Jargon:** If a 12-year-old wouldn’t get it, simplify.

---

## 9) Testing & Iteration
**Rapid A/B Grid (pick 2 to test weekly)**  
- **Niche:** [role/industry]  
- **Result:** [metric/time]  
- **Mechanism:** [process/system/tool]  
- **Objection removed:** [pain/risk]

**Acceptance Signals**
- 20–30% reply rate in cold DMs or emails  
- 2–5% landing page conversion to call  
- Prospects **repeat your pitch back** in their words  
- Fewer “so what do you actually do?” objections

**Tweak Order**
1) Result clarity → 2) Niche precision → 3) Objection → 4) Mechanism label

---

## 10) Delivery Tips
- **Lead with the result**; pause; then mechanism.  
- Keep a **calm tone**; clarity sells, speed doesn’t.  
- Finish with a **soft CTA:** “Want a quick example?” / “Want me to audit yours?”  
- Memorize **two versions**: full (20s) and hook (5s).
