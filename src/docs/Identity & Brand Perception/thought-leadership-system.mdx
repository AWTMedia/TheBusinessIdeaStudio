# 🧠 Thought Leadership — Founder Framework
*Concise, practical guide to design, publish, and sustain market-leading ideas.*

## 1) Definition
**Thought Leadership:**  
The consistent, evidence-backed articulation of *useful, original ideas* that change how a specific audience **thinks and acts**—and that your company is uniquely positioned to deliver on.

> If it doesn’t shift beliefs or decisions in your ICP, it’s content—not thought leadership.

---

## 2) Positioning Your POV (in 15 minutes)

**POV One-Liner:**  
> *In a world where [conventional belief], leaders win by [contrarian belief] because [evidence].*

**Fill-ins**
- **ICP:** Who must this change the mind of?
- **Status Quo:** What’s the default belief you’re replacing?
- **Contrarian Truth:** What do you believe instead?
- **Mechanism:** What makes your approach work?
- **Receipts:** 3 proofs (data, case, demo).

*Rule:* Specific > clever. Make it falsifiable and testable.

---

## 3) Pillars (3–5 Non-Negotiables)

For each pillar, define: **Name → Claim → Proof → Behavior → KPI**

**Example set**
1. **Evidence Over Echo** — Every claim cites original data or a field-tested mechanism.  
   - KPI: % posts with first/party data or demo ≥ 70%
2. **Teach, Don’t Tease** — Actionable frameworks beat vague inspiration.  
   - KPI: Saves/prints per post; average watch/read time
3. **Narrow to Nail** — ICP-specific problems; one market, one job-to-be-done.  
   - KPI: % content mapped to a single ICP ≥ 90%
4. **Show Your Work** — Process transparency (docs, dashboards, code, SOPs).  
   - KPI: # “open kitchen” assets shipped/month
5. **Ship Weekly** — Cadence compounds credibility.  
   - KPI: 1 flagship piece/week (or 4 shorts = 1 flagship)

---

## 4) Signature Assets (choose 1–2 to own)
- **Flagship Report:** Annual or quarterly data study w/ proprietary insights  
- **Named Framework:** 1–2 page visual model you reference everywhere  
- **Live Lab:** Public experiments, teardown streams, or case clinics  
- **Field Guide:** 20–30 page practical playbook (updated twice/year)  
- **Benchmarks Dashboard:** Ongoing metrics for your category

*Pick assets you can sustain and update; leadership is a *maintenance* game.*

---

## 5) The Publishing OS (simple & durable)

**Cadence**
- **Weekly:** 1 flagship insight (essay, video, teardown, mini-report)  
- **Daily/3×:** Clips, charts, carousels, quotes, “open kitchen” snippets

**Format Map**
- Long-form (YouTube/Article/Podcast) → sliced into  
- Mid-form (LinkedIn/Twitter/Newsletter) → sliced into  
- Short-form (IG/TikTok/Shorts) with CTA to the long-form

**Template per piece**
- *Hook → Belief shift → Framework/Steps → Case/Receipts → CTA (tool/report/demo).*

---

## 6) Proof Stack (make it unmistakable)

- **Quant:** Hard numbers (before/after, deltas, cohorts)  
- **Qual:** Named client quotes + screenshots (with permission)  
- **Demo:** Looms, sandboxes, code samples, checklists  
- **Peer:** External citations, 3rd-party features, academic refs (when relevant)

> Each flagship piece should include **at least two proofs** (Quant + Demo).

---

## 7) Distribution & Earned Reach

**Owned:** Newsletter, site/blog, YouTube, podcast  
**Rented:** LinkedIn, X, IG, TikTok, Reddit/communities  
**Borrowed:** Guest posts, AMAs, webinars, partner lists, co-reports  
**Search:** SEO pages for your framework/report; video chapters; schema markup

**Amplify Play**
- Post → DM 20 ICPs with a hyper-specific question  
- Clip into 5–7 assets; tag sources/partners; republish w/ different hooks  
- Present the same idea in **3 mediums** (text, video, diagram)

---

## 8) Measurement (leadership ≠ vanity)

**Leading Indicators**
- Saves/Forwards ratio (≥ 0.15 benchmark on social)  
- Reply rate to newsletter (≥ 3%)  
- Qualified inbound from content (booked calls per 1000 views)  
- Mentions of your *named* framework in sales calls

**Lagging Indicators**
- Close rate lift on prospects who consumed ≥2 pieces  
- Sales cycle reduction (days)  
- % pipeline sourced/assisted by content  
- Speaking invites / partner requests / citations

**Monthly Scorecard**
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Flagship pieces shipped | ≥ 4 |
| Proof artifacts added | ≥ 6 |
| Qualified inbound/bookings | MoM ↑ |
| Framework mentions (calls/DMs) | MoM ↑ |

---

## 9) 90-Day Launch Plan (simple & real)

**Days 1–7** — Draft POV & Pillars, name the framework, outline report  
**Weeks 2–4** — Ship 3 flagship pieces (teach the framework from different angles)  
**Weeks 5–8** — Run one *Live Lab* + publish mini-benchmark; guest spot x2  
**Weeks 9–12** — Release v1 Field Guide + host a webinar; collect case studies

*Every week: 1 flagship + 5–7 derivatives + 20 targeted DMs for feedback.*

---

## 10) Workflow Roles (can be 1–2 people at first)
- **Editor/Researcher:** Outline, citations, data sanity  
- **Creator:** Writes/records and hosts live sessions  
- **Ops/Repurposer:** Clips, carousels, captions, SEO, posting  
- **PM:** Calendar, briefs, analytics, distribution partners

---

## 11) Failure Modes (avoid these traps)
- **Generalism:** “For everyone” = for no one  
- **Opinion-Only:** No data, no demos, no cases  
- **Inconsistent Cadence:** Trust decays between posts  
- **Framework Flopping:** New model every month; never compounding one  
- **Vanity Metrics:** Chasing views without ICP movement

---

## 12) Templates (copy/paste)

**POV One-Liner**  
> Most [ICP] believe [status quo]. We’ve found [contrarian truth]. Here’s the data and the model we use to get [result].

**Framework Card**  
- Name (3–4 words):  
- Problem it resolves:  
- 3–5 steps or components:  
- Tool/demo link:  
- Case/metric:  

**Flagship Outline**  
1) Hook (belief to replace)  
2) Evidence (chart or case)  
3) Framework (visual + steps)  
4) Application (checklist)  
5) Case teardown  
6) CTA (report/demo/template)

**Live Lab Agenda (45m)**  
- 5m: belief shift  
- 10m: framework  
- 15m: teardown/demo  
- 10m: Q&A  
- 5m: CTA + resources

---

## 13) Final Rule
> Thought leadership is **maintenance of a useful idea** in public.  
Ship weekly. Prove relentlessly. Name your frameworks. Let the market repeat you.
