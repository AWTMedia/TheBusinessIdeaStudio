# 📘 AWT Media — Niche & Keyword Brand Document  

## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** AWT Media  
**Industry:** Social Media Marketing, Paid Ads, and Creative Strategy  
**Founded:** 2023  
**Headquarters:** Cape Town, South Africa  
**Founder:** Alexander Thomas  

**Core Identity:**  
AWT Media is a results-driven digital agency that helps **eCommerce and info-product brands** turn proven organic content into high-performing paid advertising systems.  
We combine **content psychology, cohort testing, and paid media expertise** to achieve consistent, measurable ROI.

> “We turn content that works — into content that scales.”

---

## 2. Mission & Philosophy

**Mission Statement:**  
To help visionary brands turn attention into revenue by engineering paid systems from proven organic performance.

**Brand Philosophy:**  
- **Proof over promises** — every campaign is backed by data.  
- **People-first marketing** — we scale content that emotionally resonates.  
- **Iterate, test, refine** — data-driven creativity never stops evolving.  

**Core Promise:**  
A minimum **2.2x ROAS guarantee** built on validated content, precise targeting, and full-funnel optimization.

## 3. Target Audience & Niche Definition

### 🎯 Primary Market:
- **Ecommerce brands** generating consistent organic engagement but plateauing in growth.  
- **Info-product creators** (coaches, course builders, experts) who have viral content but no paid structure.  

### 👥 Ideal Client Profile:
| Category | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Revenue Range** | $10K–$300K/month |
| **Pain Point** | Great content, poor conversions or ad ROI |
| **Goal** | Predictable, profitable customer acquisition |
| **Platform Focus** | Meta (Instagram + Facebook), TikTok, YouTube |
| **Mindset** | Data-aware, content-rich, growth-oriented |

### 🧭 Sub-Niche Opportunities:
- DTC Health & Wellness brands  
- Fitness & Lifestyle coaches  
- Digital course creators  
- Home & lifestyle ecommerce stores  
- Personal brands scaling via Skool, Kajabi, or Shopify
