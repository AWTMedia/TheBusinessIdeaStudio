# 🧭 Brand Positioning & Pillars — Founder Framework

## 0) What This Is
A fast, founder-friendly system to:
1) **Define positioning** (where you sit in the market and why you win)  
2) **Codify pillars** (the 3–5 non-negotiable truths your brand stands on)  
3) **Operationalize** both into messaging, offers, and behaviors you can measure

> If it doesn’t change customer behavior, it’s not positioning — it’s poetry.

---

## 1) Positioning — One-Page Blueprint

### 1.1 Positioning Statement (Fill-In)
> **For** [ICP], **who struggle with** [pain/outcome gap], **we are** the [category] **that** [core promise/outcome], **because** [unique proof/differentiator]. **Unlike** [competitor/alt], **we** [onlyness].

**Example (generic):**  
For founder-led DTC brands who plateau after organic growth, we are the performance studio that turns proven content into scalable paid systems, because we start from proof, not guesswork. Unlike general SMMAs, we run a creative testing OS tied to unit economics.

### 1.2 Positioning Canvas (Complete These)
- **ICP (who):**  
  - Segment:  
  - Stage/Size:  
  - Jobs-to-be-done:  
  - Buying triggers/risks:
- **Category (what are you):** Keep it simple and recognizable.  
- **Problem (why now):** Put the cost of inaction in numbers.  
- **Outcome (what changes):** Time saved, revenue gained, risk reduced.  
- **Onlyness (why you):** 2–3 things competitors won’t/can’t do.  
- **Reasons to Believe (proof):** Case metrics, mechanisms, credentials.  
- **Price Logic:** Why your price is rational vs. alternatives.  
- **No-Fit Criteria:** Who you are *not* for.

### 1.3 10-Word Test
Explain your value in **≤10 words** (no jargon).  
> “We [verb] [ICP] get [specific outcome] by [unique mechanism].”

## 2) Brand Pillars — The Non-Negotiables

### 2.1 Pillar Structure (Template x 3–5)
- **Pillar Name (1–3 words):**  
- **Founder Promise (1 sentence):**  
- **Customer Proof (evidence):** metric, case, demo, mechanism  
- **Operating Behaviors:** what the team does every time  
- **Anti-Behaviors (Guardrails):** what you refuse to do  
- **Signals & KPIs:** how we know this pillar is lived (quant/qual)

**Example Pillar:**  
- **Proof Over Promises**  
  - Promise: Data drives decisions, not opinions.  
  - Proof: Weekly test logs, ROAS board, public case summaries.  
  - Behaviors: Ship 3 experiments/week; kill losers in 72h.  
  - Anti: No launches without baseline and success criteria.  
  - KPIs: # tests/week, % budget on proven angles, win rate.

### 2.2 Pillar Checklist
- Is it **actionable** (not a platitude)?  
- Is it **differentiating** (would a rival refuse it)?  
- Is it **measurable** (has a KPI)?  
- Is it **visible** to customers (shows up in delivery and comms)?

## 3) Messaging from Positioning (Concise System)

### 3.1 Messaging Pillars (External)
Create **3–4 message themes** mapped from your pillars:
- **Outcome Claim:** The business result you reliably create.  
- **Mechanism Claim:** The “how” (your OS, process, tech).  
- **Risk Reversal:** Guarantees, payback logic, SLAs.  
- **Proof Narrative:** Case studies, before/after, numbers.

### 3.2 Copy Blocks (Fill-Ins)
- **Homepage H1:** *We [verb] [ICP] get [outcome] with [mechanism].*  
- **Subhead:** *Faster learning, lower risk, clearer decisions.*  
- **Elevator (20–30s):** Problem → Onlyness → Proof → Next step.  
- **CTA:** Low-friction, outcome-titled (e.g., “Get a Proof Audit”).

---

## 4) Validation — Make It True in the Wild

### 4.1 Smoke Tests (Low-Cost)
- Landing page + waitlist (track CVR)  
- Founder DM/testing script (10–20 conversations)  
- Offer one-pager → 10 sales calls → win/loss notes  
- Price elasticity: quote 3 tiers; record objections/pay rates

### 4.2 Acceptance Criteria
- **Message–Market Fit:** ≥30% reply rate to targeted outreach *or* ≥2–5% LP CVR  
- **Offer Pull:** ≥20% of calls request proposal  
- **Price Confidence:** ≥50% accept target pricing without discount  
- **Proof Loop:** 1 measurable win within 30 days of delivery

If not met → refine ICP, problem cost, mechanism clarity, or proof.

---

## 5) Operationalizing Positioning & Pillars

### 5.1 Build the “Receipts” Folder
- Before/after metrics, screenshots, Looms, testimonials, raw data  
- Convert to **3 proof artifacts/month** (case briefs, charts, clips)

### 5.2 Rituals (Make It Habit)
- **Weekly:** 3 tests shipped; pillar KPI review  
- **Monthly:** Win/loss analysis; refresh elevator; publish 1 case  
- **Quarterly:** Re-run positioning canvas; prune messaging

### 5.3 Scorecard (Monthly)
| Dimension | Question | Green |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Can every team member state the 10-word value? | 100% |
| Differentiation | Can a prospect repeat your onlyness? | 70%+ |
| Proof | New public proof artifacts shipped? | ≥3 |
| Consistency | % assets aligned to pillars | ≥90% |
| Conversion | LP CVR / Call→Close vs last month | Up or stable |

## 6) 90-Minute Founder Workshop (Run This)

**Part A — Positioning (45m)**  
1) Fill positioning statement (10m)  
2) ICP + Problem cost (15m)  
3) Onlyness & proof (20m)

**Part B — Pillars (30m)**  
4) Pick 3–5; write behaviors/KPIs (20m)  
5) Draft anti-behaviors (10m)

**Part C — Messaging (15m)**  
6) H1, elevator, CTA

Ship a one-pager to the team the same day.

---

## 7) Common Failure Modes (Avoid)

- **Platitude Pillars:** “Innovation, Integrity, Excellence” (unprovable)  
- **Category Mush:** Inventing new labels customers don’t search for  
- **Proof Drought:** Claims without receipts  
- **Audience Creep:** Serving everyone; resonating with no one  
- **No Guardrails:** Saying yes to work that breaks pillars
